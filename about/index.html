I built this for my own family first.

A.Point Advisory is a workshop, not an agency and not a template shop. It builds Merkhet, proves it in a real business, and installs it for other makers.

  1. 1

    It started with a real problem

    My wife makes cakes to order at home in Doha, in small batches, six days a week. The orders came in through WhatsApp. The stock lived in her head. And neither of us could have told you, honestly, what a month actually earned.

  2. 2

    So I built the thing that was missing

    A site that takes orders and sends them straight into WhatsApp, where her customers already were. Then a private app for the stock, the schedule and the real numbers. Then the accounting that turns "money came in" into "this is what is left".

  3. 3

    Then reality broke it, repeatedly

    A price changed and rewrote last month. A photo covered the text on one phone and not another. A box promised a cake it no longer contained. Every one of those became a rule, and an automated check that makes sure it never happens again. There are 3,400+ of them now.

  4. 4

    That is what is for sale

    Not an idea, not a demo. The system that survived all of it, installed and run for you. The mistakes are already caught — you get the version that comes after them.

The proof is public.

It sells in Qatari riyals, delivers across Doha, works six days a week and closes on Friday. Browse it like a customer — but please do not send a test order. It is a real business.

What we will never do.

Sell you hours

There is no hourly rate here. You buy a system and a result, at a price you can read before we speak.

Build one-offs

The system is the product. If what you need would help every business on it, it goes into the system and everyone gets it. If it is only for you, it is quoted separately.

Discount to win you

Not for anyone, not for a second brand, not to go faster. What is free is the call, the recommendation, and the guarantee.

Invent a testimonial

There is one client so far, and it is my own family. When there are more, you will hear their words, not mine.

Hold your data hostage

Export it whenever you like. If you leave, we hand over everything.

Use your customers as a demo

Never. The shop shown on this site is fictional, and every number in it is sample data.
