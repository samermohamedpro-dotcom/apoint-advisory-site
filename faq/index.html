The price

€249 a month, setup €3,900, or €2,490 a year — do I pay both?

No — never both. There are two payments, and only two. The setup fee, paid once, in two halves. Then the subscription, which is either €249 every month OR €2,490 once a year — ten months' worth, so paying yearly gives you two months free, and the yearly price is not refunded if you stop early. You choose one or the other. The pricing page shows the first-year total both ways.

Why is there a setup fee at all?

Because the first month is where the real work is: your products, prices, photos, delivery zones and hours go into the system, your site and back office are built around them, and everything is tested before you go live. The subscription pays for running it afterwards — hosting, updates, backups, support — not for building it. Charging setup separately is what keeps the monthly price low.

Can I pay the setup fee in two parts?

Yes: half when you say yes, half the day you go live. If we miss the 30-day guarantee, everything you have paid for setup comes back to you.

Why does the price go up after the third client?

Because the price follows the proof. The first three clients take a system that has run in one business so far, and they keep their price for life. The ones after buy something that has proved itself again. Prices go up with time; they never come down.

Is my advertising budget included?

No. Marketing is a separate subscription, with a waiting list for now — and even then, the money that goes to Instagram or Google is yours. A domain name costs about €10–15 a year, paid directly to the registrar, in your name.

Getting started

How long until I'm live?

It depends on how complete your materials are when they reach me, so I do not quote a generic delay. The day we have your seven items, I give you your go-live date in writing, and it is never more than 30 days. If we miss it, the setup fee comes back.

What do you need from me?

Seven things: your product list with prices — a photo of a list is fine; one to three photos per product, natural light, no filter; where you deliver and what you charge; your opening days, hours and time zone; the WhatsApp number that should receive orders; your currency; and your logo and colours if you have them. If you do not, we start from a clean base.

What if I'm late sending my photos?

Then the clock has not started. The 30 days count from the day I have everything — that is the only fair way to do it. Send what you have when you have it, and I will tell you the exact day the countdown begins.

I'm not technical. Is this going to be hard?

You will never touch code, hosting or settings. You get an app on your phone with your products, your orders and your numbers. Everything underneath is our job.

The product

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. Your customers need nothing new: they open your site in their browser, choose, and the order opens in their WhatsApp, ready to send. The private app is for you only.

Does it take card payments online?

No, on purpose. Orders are confirmed on WhatsApp and you get paid the way you already do — bank transfer, cash on delivery, your local payment app. No card gateway means no commission on every order, no chargebacks, no account to be approved for, and it works in every country.

Do I need my own domain name?

Yes, and it is registered in your name from the first day — about €10–15 a year, paid directly to the registrar. I set it up for you. If you ever leave, it leaves with you.

Can I change a price or add a product myself?

Yes — from the app on your phone. It is on your site within a minute.

Where you are

Do I need to be in Europe or the Gulf?

No. If your customers use WhatsApp, it works. There is no payment integration and no marketplace approval, so there is nothing country-specific to block you. Your setup is in your currency and your time zone.

Does it work in my language?

Your setup includes one language. A second is €900 more at setup and €20 more a month, because every product, price and caption then lives twice, for good; you provide the translations of your products, and we handle everything else. The system runs today in English and Arabic; for any other language, ask me before counting on it.

Does it work in my currency?

Any currency, and any time zone. They are set once, for you, at setup.

Trust

Who owns the site and the data?

Your work is yours: products, recipes, photos, customers, orders, sales and accounts, in open formats, plus your domain name and your accounts, which are in your name from the start. The machinery — the code, the engine, the app, the templates — stays ours; you use it as long as you subscribe. That is what makes it possible to keep running and improving it for everyone on it.

Can I see it working?

Yes. A registered home bakery in Doha, Qatar runs on this exact system, and its site is public. Browse it like a customer: pick a box, see what is available today, open the order sheet. Please do not send a test order — it is a real business.

Do you have other clients?

Honestly: one, and it is the bakery this was built for and refined on for months of daily orders. You would be among the first three outside clients — the founding clients, who keep their price for life. That is also why the guarantee exists: if your site is not live within 30 days, you do not pay for setup.

What happens if I want to stop?

You cancel and the billing stops. Everything stays online until the end of the period you paid for, your export goes out before anything is switched off, and your domain and accounts are already in your name.

Do you ever give a discount?

No — not for anyone, not for a second brand, not to go faster. What we give instead is access: a second brand does not wait in the queue, it takes the next free setup slot. The call, the written recommendation and the guarantee are free.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. Up to two months a year, in one or two goes. Your subscription stops, nothing is deleted, and your site takes no orders until you're back.

Will you show me how it works?

Yes. A walkthrough the day you go live, then as many short calls as you need for the first 30 days. After that, WhatsApp.

How quickly do you answer?

WhatsApp is always open. I read every message myself. You'll usually have an answer within a few hours — and never more than one night.
