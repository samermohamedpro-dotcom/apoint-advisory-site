What happens when a client books

  1. 1

    The client chooses

    On your request page, in six languages. One question per screen: the day, how long, which car. The price shows as they go.

  2. 2

    It reaches you

    In your app, under Requests received. If your number is on the page, it also opens in WhatsApp, already written.

  3. 3

    You make it a mission

    One tap. Choose the chauffeur and the car. If either is already booked at that time, the app says so before you promise anything.

  4. 4

    The chauffeur gets the brief

    Already written, sent from your WhatsApp in one tap: the time, the place, the flight. Never the price the client pays.

  5. 5

    The ride is over

    In the chauffeur's own space, by private link: start, finish, extra hours, fuel, tolls. Nothing changes on the mission until you apply it.
