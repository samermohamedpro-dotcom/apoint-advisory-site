Who this is for, and who it isn't.

This is for you if

  • You make things to eat, to order, and sell through WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • Orders come in — or are about to — and keeping track of them is getting hard.
  • You want one honest picture of your numbers, on your phone.
  • You would rather own your customers than rent them from a platform.

Probably not, if

  • You mainly want to be listed on a delivery marketplace.
  • You sell over a counter, with a till and staff on the floor.
  • You would rather build and host it yourself.
  • You need online card payment on day one.

If you're getting fewer than about ten orders a month, don't buy this yet — it won't pay for itself. Come back when you do. I'll still be here.
